# **App Name**: HackTrack

## Core Features:

- Event Schedule: Display a detailed schedule of the hackathon, including workshops, talks, judging times, and social events.
- Registration: Allow participants to register for the hackathon and create profiles.
- Live Updates & Announcements: Provide a real-time feed of announcements, schedule changes, and important updates during the event.
- Submission Platform: Enable teams to submit their projects with descriptions, links, and any required documentation.
- Judging and Feedback System: Enable judges to view project submissions, assign scores based on pre-defined criteria, and provide feedback, which the AI will then summarize.
- Team Formation: A matchmaking board will connect individuals into teams based on skillset.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep indigo (#4B0082), conveying sophistication and innovation.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F0F0), provides a clean, modern backdrop.
- Accent color: Electric violet (#8F00FF), adds a dynamic, energetic touch to highlights and CTAs.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, for a techy, scientific feel.
- Body font: 'Inter', sans-serif, for modern readability and clarity.
- Use modern, line-style icons to represent different event sections and actions.
- Emphasize clear information hierarchy with distinct sections and card-based design for readability.
- Subtle animations on button hovers and loading states to enhance interactivity.
